AI built for how your industry works

Every sector has its own data, its own compliance pressure and its own definition of a good outcome. Here's what we build for six of them.

Healthcare & Clinics

Give clinicians their time back

Documentation burden, scheduling inefficiency and inconsistent follow-up all quietly erode both clinician time and patient experience.

Challenges we see

  • Hours lost daily to charting and coding
  • No-shows and scheduling inefficiency
  • Inconsistent patient follow-up

What we build

PractiGO for ambient documentation and follow-up automation, plus custom integrations into practice management systems. Capabilities include AI-generated visit summaries, no-show prediction and privacy-conscious data pipelines.

Finance & Fintech

Automate the back office, safely

Financial teams need automation that is fast and auditable in equal measure — every decision needs a trail.

Challenges we see

  • Manual reconciliation and document review
  • Slow, inconsistent risk and compliance checks
  • Customer support volume that scales faster than staff

What we build

Document intelligence and RAG assistants over policy and compliance documents, automated reconciliation workflows with full audit trails, and agentic support triage with human sign-off on anything customer-facing.

Retail & E-commerce

Personalize without the guesswork

Retail teams sit on huge amounts of transaction and behavior data that rarely gets turned into action fast enough.

Challenges we see

  • Generic product and content recommendations
  • Slow, manual product content creation
  • Support tickets that outpace headcount

What we build

AI-generated product descriptions and merchandising content, customer support copilots trained on your catalog and policies, and demand-forecasting pipelines built on your own sales data.

Logistics

Turn tracking data into action

Logistics operations generate constant streams of data — the value is in acting on it before a delay becomes a problem.

Challenges we see

  • Manual exception handling on delayed shipments
  • Fragmented data across carriers and systems
  • Reactive rather than predictive routing

What we build

Automated exception detection and routing agents, data pipelines that unify carrier feeds into one view, and predictive ETA models that flag risk before a delay happens.

Education

Support every learner, at scale

Institutions want personalized support for students and less administrative load on staff — often with limited budget for either.

Challenges we see

  • Administrative workload on faculty and staff
  • Limited one-on-one time for student support
  • Content that needs to scale across many courses

What we build

AI teaching assistants that answer course-specific questions from your own materials, automated grading-support tools with human final review, and admissions and enrollment workflow automation.

Real Estate

Move leads to close, faster

Real estate teams juggle listings, leads and paperwork across too many disconnected tools.

Challenges we see

  • Slow lead response and follow-up
  • Manual listing description and document work
  • Disconnected CRM, listing and document tools

What we build

Lead-response agents that qualify and route inquiries instantly, AI-generated listing content, and document automation that connects your CRM to contracts and disclosures.

Don't See Your Industry?

We build for more than these six

If your work involves data, documents or repeatable decisions, there's likely an AI use case worth exploring together.
