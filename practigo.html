Our Own Product

The AI SaaS platform built for doctors

PractiGO listens, drafts and organizes clinical work so physicians spend their time with patients instead of paperwork — ambient documentation, visit summaries, scheduling intelligence and follow-up automation in one secure platform.

Book a Demo See Pricing

The Problem

Doctors lose hours a day to documentation

Charting, coding and after-visit summaries routinely eat into evenings and weekends. Studies across health systems consistently point to documentation burden as a leading driver of physician burnout — time that could go to patients instead goes to a keyboard.

PractiGO was built to close that gap: it captures the visit, structures the note, and surfaces what needs a human decision — so clinicians review and approve rather than transcribe from scratch.

How It Works

From visit to record in four steps

1

Capture

PractiGO listens to the visit (with consent) or accepts dictated notes.

2

Draft

It structures a draft note, summary and coding suggestions for review.

3

Review

The clinician edits and approves — nothing is finalized without sign-off.

4

Follow up

Automated reminders, summaries and scheduling keep the patient on track.

Product FAQ

No. PractiGO is a documentation and workflow assistant. It does not diagnose, treat or make clinical decisions — those remain with the treating physician at all times.

Data is encrypted in transit and at rest, access is role-based, and every action is logged. The architecture is built with healthcare privacy requirements in mind, using a HIPAA-conscious design.

Integration depends on your EHR's available APIs. We assess compatibility during onboarding and support common interoperability standards where the EHR vendor makes them available.
