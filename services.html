Generative AI Solutions

We build LLM-powered products that write, summarize, search and reason over your own data — private assistants, customer-facing copilots and content engines that stay grounded in your source material rather than the open internet.

Every build includes evaluation and guardrails from day one: we measure accuracy against real examples, add hallucination controls, and keep an eye on inference cost so the system stays affordable as usage grows.

  • Private RAG assistants over your documents
  • Customer-facing copilots and chat interfaces
  • Automated document and report generation
  • Model selection, benchmarking and evaluation
  • Guardrails and hallucination controls

What you get: a working, evaluated GenAI product your team can operate and extend.

Agentic AI Systems

Agents that don't just answer — they act. We design multi-step autonomous systems with tool access, memory and defined boundaries, so they can complete real operational work instead of stopping at a suggestion.

Human-in-the-loop checkpoints are built in wherever a decision carries real risk, and every action is logged, so your team keeps oversight without having to babysit the system.

  • Multi-agent orchestration and coordination
  • Tool and API calling with defined permissions
  • Human-in-the-loop approval checkpoints
  • Full audit trails of agent decisions and actions

What you get: an autonomous system that runs routine work while you keep control of what matters.

AI Automation

We map the manual work draining your team's hours — intake, triage, data entry, reconciliation, reporting and follow-up — and automate it end to end, connecting the systems you already use rather than replacing them.

Automations are built to fail safely: exceptions get routed to a human instead of silently breaking, and every run is logged so you can see exactly what happened.

  • Workflow discovery and process mapping
  • Document and email automation
  • CRM and ERP system integration
  • Exception handling and human hand-off

What you get: measurably fewer manual hours, with a clear log of what the automation did.

Cloud Services

Cloud architecture built for AI workloads. We design, migrate and operate secure, scalable infrastructure across AWS, Azure and GCP, matched to your existing environment rather than a single preferred vendor.

Cost governance is built in from day one — right-sizing, autoscaling policy and usage alerts — so infrastructure spend stays predictable as workloads grow.

  • Cloud migration and modernization
  • Kubernetes and serverless architecture
  • CI/CD pipelines and DevOps practice
  • Cost optimization and FinOps reporting

What you get: infrastructure that scales with usage and a bill that doesn't surprise you.

Web Design & Development

Fast, accessible, conversion-focused websites and web applications. Custom design and clean semantic code, built to real Core Web Vitals targets rather than a generic template.

We set up CMS or headless options depending on how your team publishes, so content updates don't require a developer for every change.

  • Custom UI/UX design
  • Responsive, accessible front-end builds
  • SEO and performance engineering
  • CMS and headless CMS setups

What you get: a fast, accessible site your team can maintain and that performs in search.

AI Consulting & Strategy

Not sure where AI actually pays off? We run a structured discovery across your operations, score candidate use cases on impact versus effort, and deliver a costed roadmap.

Every roadmap ships with a working proof of concept for the top use case — not a slide deck — so you can see real output before committing further budget.

  • AI readiness assessment
  • Use-case discovery and prioritization
  • Proof of concept delivered in weeks
  • Adoption planning and team training

What you get: a costed, prioritized roadmap plus proof it will actually work.

Custom AI Development

Custom model integration, fine-tuning and application development built around your data and your stack. We take a use case from prototype to a system your team relies on daily.

We favor proven foundation models with targeted fine-tuning over building from scratch, which keeps cost and time-to-value realistic.

  • Model fine-tuning and prompt architecture
  • Custom application development
  • API design and backend engineering
  • Testing, evaluation and staged rollout

What you get: a production application your team owns outright.

Data Engineering & RAG

Clean data pipelines and retrieval-augmented architectures that ground AI output in your actual documents and data, rather than whatever a model happened to be trained on.

This is often the highest-leverage work in a GenAI project — good retrieval quietly fixes more hallucination problems than a bigger model does.

  • Data pipeline design and ETL
  • Vector database and retrieval architecture
  • Data quality and freshness monitoring
  • Access controls on sensitive data

What you get: AI answers grounded in your real, current data.

AI Integration

We connect AI capabilities into the tools you already use — CRM, support desk, ERP, internal portals — so new intelligence shows up inside existing workflows instead of a separate app nobody opens.

Integration work includes authentication, error handling and monitoring, so the connection stays reliable after launch, not just on demo day.

  • API and webhook integration
  • CRM, helpdesk and ERP connectors
  • Single sign-on and permission mapping
  • Monitoring and error alerting

What you get: AI that lives inside the tools your team already opens every day.

Managed AI Support

Ongoing monitoring, model updates and tuning after launch, so performance doesn't quietly drift as your data and usage patterns change.

We track accuracy, latency and cost against agreed thresholds and flag issues before they become visible to your users.

  • Performance and cost monitoring
  • Model and prompt updates over time
  • Incident response and SLA options
  • Monthly performance reporting

What you get: a system that keeps performing without you having to watch it.

Staff Augmentation

Senior AI engineers embedded directly in your team for the duration of a project, working in your tools, your process and your standups — not a black-box outsourced team.

Useful when you have the roadmap but need extra hands, or when a specific AI skill set isn't yet on your team.

  • Senior AI/ML engineers, embedded
  • Flexible engagement length
  • Works inside your existing tools and process
  • Clean documentation and knowledge transfer

What you get: senior AI capacity, without a lengthy hiring process.

Healthcare AI

Deep domain focus from building our own clinical product, PractiGO. We bring that operator experience to hospitals, clinic chains and health-tech companies.

Work spans clinical documentation, patient engagement automation, and privacy-conscious data pipelines — always with the understanding that clinical judgment stays with the clinician.

  • Clinical documentation AI
  • Patient engagement and reminder automation
  • Interoperability and data-exchange support
  • Privacy-first data architecture

What you get: healthcare AI built by a team that operates its own clinical product.
